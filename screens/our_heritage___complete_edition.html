Our Origin

Roots in the Sahel

Born from the sun-drenched coastal plains of the Tunisian Sahel, OLIV'OR is more than a brand; it is a testament to three thousand years of Mediterranean heritage. Our story begins in the ancient groves where the air carries the scent of salt and earth.

Tunisia is the world's leading orchard of organic olives, a land where trees are treated as family members and the harvest is a sacred dance. We founded OLIV'OR to bring this unadulterated excellence—once reserved for local masters—to the world's most discerning tables.

A Timeless Journey

1894

First Grove Planted

The Ben Salem family establishes our founding orchard in the Sousse region, planting the resilient Chemlali saplings that form the heart of our legacy.

1950

Modern Pressing

Transitioning from stone mills to early hydraulic pressing, allowing for cleaner, more consistent oil extraction while maintaining the soul of the fruit.

2024

Global Recognition

Launching our boutique label worldwide, combining ancestral wisdom with nitrogen-blanketed storage and sustainable luxury packaging.

The Path to Perfection

From the tree to your table, our process is defined by speed, precision, and profound respect for the fruit.

1. Hand Harvest

Selective hand-picking at the peak of ripeness to avoid bruising.

2. Cold Extraction

Extracted within 24 hours at temperatures below 27°C to preserve nutrients.

3. Natural Settling

Clarified through natural sedimentation in temperature-controlled tanks.

workspace_premium
4. Bottling

Protected from light and oxygen in our signature dark glass bottles.

The Faces of the Grove

"We don't just harvest fruit; we cultivate a legacy. Every tree in this grove is a member of our family."

— Ahmed Ben Salem, Master Cultivator

Ahmed represents the third generation of the Ben Salem family to walk these groves. His hands, weathered by decades of sun and earth, hold the intuitive wisdom that no machine can replicate.

At OLIV'OR, our partnership with local farmers is rooted in fair-trade principles. By investing directly in their livelihoods, we ensure that the soul of Tunisia remains vibrant in every bottle.
